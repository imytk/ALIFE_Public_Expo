About This Expo

Welcome to the Artificial Life Expo, an online museum exploring the field of Artificial Life (ALife): the science of building and studying life-like systems from the ground up using computers, robots, chemistry, and more. This expo was first presented as a physical exhibition at the ALIFE 2025 conference in Kyoto, Japan (6–10 October 2025), and has since grown into this permanent digital experience.

The expo is designed for anyone curious about ALife — whether you're completely new to the field or an existing ALife researcher who wants to explore areas outside your own specialism. It's a broad overview: nine zones covering everything from the foundational question of what life actually is, through core principles like emergence, self-organisation, and evolution, to the diverse forms ALife takes in practice — digital, robotic, and chemical — and the big societal and philosophical questions the field raises. Each zone combines short videos, explanatory text, and live interactive simulations you can play with directly in your browser.

No prior knowledge is required. Curiosity is your only ticket.

If you'd like to go deeper, we recommend exploring these community resources:

  • ALife Encyclopedia — a growing open reference for the field
  • ALife Newsletter — the community's regular digest of research and events
  • ISAL Website — the International Society for Artificial Life

This expo is constantly evolving. The version you see here is already more expansive than the original physical exhibition at ALIFE 2025, and we plan to keep growing it. If you've spotted an error, feel something is missing, or have a suggestion, we'd genuinely love to hear from you. We're also actively looking for volunteer translators to help make this expo accessible in more languages over time. If you're interested in translating content into your language and helping maintain it, please get in touch.
