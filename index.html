About This Expo

Welcome to Life As It Could Be — a digital companion to the ALIFE 2025 Public Expo, originally presented at the Artificial Life 2025 conference. This online museum invites you to explore the field of Artificial Life (ALife): the science of building life-like systems from the ground up using computers, robots, chemistry, and more.

Each zone of this expo introduces you to a new facet of ALife — from foundational principles like emergence and evolution, to the big philosophical questions about what life actually is. Along the way you can watch videos, play with live simulations, and discover how ALife research is shaping our world.

No prior knowledge is required. Curiosity is your only ticket.
