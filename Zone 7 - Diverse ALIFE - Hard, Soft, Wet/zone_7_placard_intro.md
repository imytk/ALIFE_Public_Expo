# The Many Faces of Artificial Life

So far, we've explored _what_ Artificial Life is and how it operates using principles like emergence and evolution. Now, let's talk about the variety of forms that Artificial Life can take, pushing the boundaries of where "life" can manifest.

Just as biological life exists in countless species across diverse ecosystems, Artificial Life researchers create and study "life" in a range of distinct mediums and approaches. These different forms allow us to explore different facets of life's fundamental properties:

- Hard ALife: This involves creating physical machines and robots that exhibit life-like behaviors. These "embodied" systems interact with the real world, often drawing inspiration from biology for their form, movement, and collective intelligence.

- Soft ALife: Here, life is expressed purely in the digital realm – as software, algorithms, and simulations. It allows us to build entire virtual worlds and evolve complex behaviors at astonishing speeds.

- Wet ALife: This is perhaps the most audacious frontier, attempting to create life-like properties using chemical and biological materials in the laboratory. It explores the very origins of life and the possibility of building new forms of biology from the ground up.
