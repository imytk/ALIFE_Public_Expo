Embed compatibility tester

Each candidate below is loaded in an iframe exactly the way the expo zones load their interactives. Sites that send X-Frame-Options or a frame-ancestors CSP will refuse to appear — that is the "refused to connect" error. Open this file in your browser (double-click it) and read the badges.

The badge is a best-effort automatic guess. Trust your eyes over the badge: if the panel below shows a working simulation, it embeds. Some sites load but are unusable at this size — note those too.
